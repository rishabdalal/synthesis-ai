Know how your customers will react before you ship.

Synthesis builds synthetic customers trained on your own data. See your real customer journey, change a lever like price, trial length or an offer, and get the likely outcome with odds and the evidence behind it.

Request early access For product, growth and pricing teams.

How it works

  1. Connect your data

    Read-only connections to Postgres, Snowflake, S3, Delta tables and tools like Stripe. Nothing is installed in your systems.

  2. See your real journey

    Your funnel is built from your own events: every step, where customers drop off, and the customer types you actually have.

  3. Pull a lever, see the outcome

    Thousands of synthetic customers walk your journey with the change applied. You get the result, the odds, and which customers win or lose.

Not a chatbot pretending to be a customer

Grounded in what your customers did

Every synthetic decision is checked against how similar real customers behaved, and you can see the evidence for any choice.

Odds, not one guess

Each experiment runs many times and reports a range, the chance it wins, and the downside risk.

Accuracy you can check

We replay your past A/B tests and show how often Synthesis would have called the result.

Your data stays yours

An isolated environment per company, read-only access you can revoke anytime, and your data only ever trains your model.
